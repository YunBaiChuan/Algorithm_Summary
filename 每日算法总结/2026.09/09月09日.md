## 第一题：统计范围内的逗号 II https://leetcode.cn/problems/count-commas-in-range-ii/?envType=daily-question&envId=2026-09-09

**思路：分类讨论。直接分类讨论就可以做到$O(1)$，不是非常复杂**

**Python代码：**
```python
class Solution:
    def countCommas(self, n: int) -> int:
        ans = 0 

        if n == 10 ** 15:
            ans += 5 
            n = 10 ** 15 - 1
        
        if 10 ** 12 <= n < 10 ** 15:
            ans += (n - 10 ** 12 + 1) * 4 
            n = 10 ** 12 - 1
        
        if 10 ** 9 <= n < 10 ** 12:
            ans += (n - 10 ** 9 + 1) * 3 
            n = 10 ** 9 - 1

        if 10 ** 6 <= n < 10 ** 9:
            ans += (n - 10 ** 6 + 1) * 2 
            n = 10 ** 6 - 1

        if 1000 <= n < 10 ** 6:
            ans += (n - 1000 + 1) * 1

        if n < 1000:
            ans += 0 
            
        return ans
```
